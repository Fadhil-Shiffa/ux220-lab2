Something I Read Lately

Recently, I read an article called "How to Survive the North Sentinel Island" from What If. The article is about the North Sentinel Island, which is a remote island in the Bay of Bengal that is home to the Sentinelese people. They have remained almost completely isolated from the outside world, which immediately made the article interesting to me.

One of the most interesting parts was learning about previous real-world attempts to contact the Sentinelese. For example, in 1991, a group of anthropologists successfully approached the island peacefully by floating coconuts over to the residents. The text also discusses John Chau, an American missionary who tried several times to enter the island in 2018 even after being forced away

I found this article interesting because it is strange to think that a group of people can still live almost completely separated from the modern world and still survive. I was also surprised by how dangerous attempts to contact them have been. And how their worst enemy could be us because of viruses we carry, since their immune system is weaker Reading this has motivated me to learn more about the history and preservation of uncontacted tribes
